I’m Euan Baldwin, a robotics and physics graduate

I'm a First-Class MSc Robotics and BSc Physics graduate working in AI safety operations. I want advanced AI to be developed safely and to the benefit of everyone, and I believe I can contribute most by building and running the organisations and communities working on the problem.

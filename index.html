About Me

I'm a First-Class MSc Robotics and BSc Physics graduate working in AI safety operations. I want advanced AI to be developed safely and to the benefit of everyone, and I believe I can contribute most by building and running the organisations and communities working on the problem.

Now

Events at the Centre for Effective Altruism: content and events for EA Global NYC, production coordination for the Summit on Existential Security, and room management at EA Global London.

Previously

Chair of Effective Altruism Manchester, where I ran its fellowship programmes end to end and grew the committee from 2 to 10; before that, robotics and physics at the University of Manchester.

Beyond Work

A Giving What We Can pledger since 2022, a PADI Advanced Open Water Diver, and a keen traveller. My technical background keeps me close to AI safety research, news, and debate.
